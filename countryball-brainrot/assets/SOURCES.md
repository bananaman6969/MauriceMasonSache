# Flag assets

Country names and PNGs are bundled from [Flagpedia / FlagCDN](https://flagpedia.net/download/api).
The collection includes 250 country and territory codes, including Kosovo.
EU and UN organization flags are excluded. Source images are based on Wikimedia
Commons vector flags. See the provider's documentation for attribution and source details.

`python fetch_flags.py` refreshes the assets. Normal preview/export use only local
files and do not contact the provider.
